How it works

From registration to getting paid.

The same governed pipeline the platform runs internally — nothing about your verification is faked or assumed.

1

Register & submit documents

Choose your role and provide your licence, registration, and — for fleets and operators — your LTFRB authority and permits.

2

We verify — for real

Our compliance team checks KYB and LTFRB authority against official sources. Nothing is marked verified without a recorded source.

3

Get matched to jobs

Once active, you receive work matched to your lanes, unit type, capacity and area of operation.

4

Get paid, protected

Customer funds are held by a regulated provider and released to you when the job is verified — not before.

Why LiftHaul
