Shared applicant requirements

These controls apply to every unit. The selected catalogue record adds its own document and matching rules.

  • Valid driver licence, government ID, and driver photograph
  • OR/CR and ownership or driver-to-vehicle authorization
  • Insurance and applicable regulatory documents
  • Front, rear, both sides, cargo area, plate, odometer, and condition photos
  • Service areas, schedule, accepted and excluded cargo
  • Verified bank or provider-supported e-wallet payout details
