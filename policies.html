Terms of Service

LiftHaul provides logistics-marketplace technology for booking, matching, verification workflows, trip evidence, communication and governed payments through approved third parties. A booking becomes binding only under its accepted quotation and service conditions.

User responsibilities

  • Provide accurate identities, cargo, route, timing and permit information.
  • Use only accounts and payment instruments the user is authorized to use.
  • Do not misrepresent a licence, franchise, vehicle, driver, payment or delivery event.
  • Retain official transaction and delivery references.

Platform controls

LiftHaul may suspend eligibility, dispatch, release or access when required documents expire, a risk or dispute hold applies, provider records conflict, or a governed review is incomplete. Final commercial terms, limitation of liability, governing law and complaint escalation require counsel approval before launch.

Privacy Policy

LiftHaul processes identity, company, contact, booking, location, vehicle, driver, document, trip-evidence, payment-reference and support data for specified booking, compliance, safety, fraud, settlement and legal purposes.

  • Workspace access is organization- and role-scoped.
  • Raw card, bank and wallet credentials are not stored in LiftHaul business records; only provider-issued aliases and masked labels are retained.
  • Government-issued licences and similar records are treated as sensitive and access-controlled.
  • Provider, mapping, messaging and hosting processors must be contractually assessed.
  • Retention, deletion, data-subject requests, cross-border processing, breach procedures and the Data Protection Officer’s production contact remain launch-gated.

Refund and Cancellation Policy

Refund eligibility follows the accepted booking, cancellation point, work already performed, provider-channel capability, dispute outcome and applicable law. A refund request does not change the payment record until verified by the payment provider.

  • Statuses: Refund Processing, Partially Refunded or Refunded.
  • Partial refunds require channel support and authorized approval.
  • Duplicate, mismatched, delayed or uncertain refunds remain Under Review.
  • Provider confirmation and daily reconciliation are required before closure.
  • Any disclosed cancellation, processing or platform fee must be shown before confirmation.

Protected-Payment Terms

“Protected Payment” describes a conditional payment workflow. It does not represent a regulated funds-holding product unless qualified Philippine counsel and the authorized financial partner approve the structure and terminology.

Core lifecycle

Awaiting Payment → Payment Processing → Funded and Protected → Service in Progress → Delivery Submitted → Awaiting Confirmation → Payment Released.

Funding verification

No click, redirect, screenshot or manually typed reference proves funding. API confirmation requires an authenticated provider notification plus a direct provider query matching reference, booking, amount, currency and successful status. Exceptional manual verification requires a separate authorized reviewer and an official provider record.

Release conditions

Driver completion alone cannot release funds. Required evidence may include timestamped pickup, geolocation, cargo photographs, recipient identity, proof of delivery, signature or one-time PIN, client confirmation, and the stated dispute or automatic-confirmation period. An eligible dispute freezes the affected unreleased amount.

Funds and fees

Customer funds must not be held in LiftHaul’s ordinary operating account. The approved partner agreement must govern custody or safeguarding, release authority, settlement, fees, chargebacks, insolvency, abandoned funds, refunds and reconciliation before live activation.
