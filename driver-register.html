Driver approval is not vehicle approval.

A licensed driver may apply without owning a unit. Before assignment, LiftHaul requires a valid relationship to the registered owner or fleet and a separately eligible vehicle.

Driver evidence

Government identity, valid professional licence, appropriate codes, recent photograph, emergency contact, safety qualifications, and service-area declarations.

Owner authorization

If the driver is not the registered owner, the responsible owner or fleet must explicitly sponsor or authorize the relationship.

Vehicle evidence

Plate uniqueness, OR/CR, insurance, condition photographs, capacity reference, inspection results, and category-specific documents.

Clear commercial expectations

Opportunities—not guaranteed income.

Availability, route demand, vehicle compatibility, customer acceptance, pricing, completion evidence, and compliance all affect whether a driver receives work and when payment becomes eligible.

Booking and earnings record

  • Review the offered route, cargo, price, and conditions before acceptance.
  • Only complete eligible work under the assigned fleet and vehicle.
  • Keep pickup, delivery, exception, and handoff evidence in the governed booking.

Protected Payment where available

  • Payment status and payout timing depend on the selected regulated payment provider.
  • Refunds, disputes, cancellations, and provider charges follow approved policies.
  • LiftHaul never describes funds as escrow without an approved legal and provider structure.

Registration questions

Use the role that reflects who owns the vehicle and who is accountable for operations.

Can I register without owning a vehicle?

Yes. Apply as a driver. Before assignment, the registered owner or fleet must authorize or sponsor the relationship and the vehicle must pass its own review.

Can one owner add several vehicles?

Yes. One verified owner or fleet account can add multiple units. Every unit keeps a separate status and evidence record.

Does approval cover passenger transport?

No. Cargo registration does not activate passenger transport. Passenger operations require a separate regulatory, insurance, and operating-compliance track.

What happens when a licence expires?

Mandatory expiry can suspend eligibility. Renewed evidence must be submitted and accepted before reactivation.

Are tanker and specialized vehicles immediately active?

No. Specialized categories need additional insurance, safety, cargo, permit, and operational review before they can be matched.

Does registration guarantee bookings?

No. Registration creates an application. Opportunities depend on approval, demand, compatibility, service areas, availability, and customer requirements.
